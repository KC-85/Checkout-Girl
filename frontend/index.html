A grassroots singer-songwriter with an instinct for honest words, bold melodies and connecting with a room.

Checkout Girl is an independent artist from Oxfordshire who thrives when her mind is focused on music in all its forms.

Her creative story includes work with UK producer ProjectP and releases through the Mustache Crew label—with plenty more still to be written.
